# Brighty — Data Analyst

**Company:** Brighty (fintech / crypto-economy app: manage, save, grow money in one app; security, innovation, transparency)
**Location:** Remote or hybrid

## The Role
Transform data into actionable insights that drive business and product decisions. Work closely with Product, Marketing, and Finance teams to uncover trends, monitor KPIs, and optimize user experience in a fast-evolving fintech and digital asset ecosystem.

## Responsibilities
- Collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data from multiple sources (app, CRM, marketing platforms, financial systems)
- Writing and optimizing MySQL and PostgreSQL queries to extract, clean, and manipulate large datasets
- Working with BigQuery for large-scale data analysis and cloud-based data warehousing
- Building and maintaining dashboards and reports in Metabase to visualize key metrics and support decision-making
- Conducting cohort analysis, user segmentation, and retention studies
- Supporting A/B testing and growth experiments with statistical insights
- Working closely with Product and Engineering teams to define data-tracking requirements and ensure data accuracy
- Presenting findings and recommendations to stakeholders in a clear, structured way

## Requirements
- University degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Computer Science, or a related field
- 2+ years of experience as a Data Analyst (fintech or product analytics experience preferred)
- Strong proficiency in SQL / MySQL
- Experience with data visualization tools (e.g. Metabase, Power BI, Tableau, Looker)
- Understanding of statistical methods, A/B testing, and data modeling
- Analytical mindset, attention to detail, and strong communication skills
- Fluency in English (written and spoken)
