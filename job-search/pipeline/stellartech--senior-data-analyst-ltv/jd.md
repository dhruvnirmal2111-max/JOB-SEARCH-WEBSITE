# StellarTech — Senior Data Analyst (LTV / Predictive Analytics)

**Company:** StellarTech — B2C mobile publisher in EdTech (subscription apps, in-app monetization, performance marketing at scale).
**Role focus:** Own and evolve the live LTV prediction algorithm. Research/predictive analytics, NOT reporting (reporting is already automated by Data Engineering). Reports to Head of Operational Department; works with Product, UA, Finance, Data Engineering.

## What you will do
- Own and develop the LTV prediction algorithm: find parameters/factors that increase forecast accuracy, prototype improvements in SQL, measure the gain, hand logic to Data Engineering.

- Research what moves LTV: product features, segments, purchase sources (e.g. CRM vs other), adapt prediction logic.
- Run analytical research end to end, from a vague question to a documented recommendation for Product/UA/Finance (payback, media-buying concentration, unit economics at company level).
