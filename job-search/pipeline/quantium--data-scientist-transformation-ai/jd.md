# Quantium — Data Scientist (Transformation AI, Quantium Telstra)

## Who is Quantium
World leader in data science and AI. Established in Australia in 2002; global team of 1,200+ across 14 locations, product + consulting. "ALL in on AI", transforming into an AI-native org, 90% weekly active AI usage, 23 years domain expertise, proprietary data partnerships.

## The Role
Data Scientist in **Transformation AI**, part of the **Quantium Telstra** team. Transformation AI builds the analytics, models and AI solutions that run inside Telstra's operational systems, supporting Telstra's Connected Future 30 strategy. Real commercial and customer impact, feeding major business decisions and powering capabilities used across Telstra daily. Design and build data science solutions, work with technical and non-technical partners to turn model outputs into actionable insights, and contribute to models running live in production.

## What You'll Do
- **Data and feature engineering:** Use Python and SQL to analyse data, engineer features, and support model development.
- **Model development:** Build, test, and improve predictive models and AI solutions used in live decisioning systems.
- **Data product development:** Take raw model outputs and transform them into data products that slot into business processes and decisioning.
- **GenAI and LLMs:** Experiment with and build GenAI/LLM capabilities, including prompting, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and agentic workflows.
- **End-to-end solution design:** Partner with stakeholders to design and deliver solutions that make model insights easy to understand, from problem definition through to deployment.
- **Delivery and improvement:** Support experimentation, validation, and ongoing improvement of solutions already in production.

## What You'll Bring
- Proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical people.
- Ability to work independently while collaborating within a team.
- A working understanding of predictive modelling (e.g. GBM), or a genuine willingness to upskill.
- Working knowledge of GenAI and LLMs (prompting, RAG, agentic workflows), or a genuine willingness to upskill.
- Curiosity and a keenness to learn in an applied analytics environment.
