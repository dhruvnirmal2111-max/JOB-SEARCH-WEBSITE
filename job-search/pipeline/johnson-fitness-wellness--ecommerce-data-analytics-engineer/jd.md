# Johnson Fitness & Wellness (Johnson Health Tech) — Ecommerce Data & Analytics Engineer

**Company:** Johnson Health Tech / Johnson Fitness & Wellness — one of the world's largest fitness-equipment manufacturers (commercial + residential brands, 60+ countries).
**Type:** Full-time. **Location:** US onsite (valid driver's license + local travel + MVR checks required).

## Position Overview
Develop and maintain the data infrastructure, reporting systems, and analytics supporting ecommerce and omnichannel business. Integrate data across ecommerce, website analytics, CRM, marketing, retail POS, ERP, and operational systems into a centralized, reliable view of business and customer performance. Build scalable pipelines + reporting; translate complex datasets into actionable insight; partner with ecommerce, digital product, marketing, CRM, retail ops, merchandising, and leadership.

## Responsibilities
**Data Engineering & Integration:** build/maintain scalable pipelines connecting ecommerce, web analytics, CRM, marketing, POS, ERP, advertising, attribution, operational systems; ETL/ELT for ingestion/transformation/normalization/reporting; reliable data models, schemas, reporting standards; integrate APIs + third-party sources; monitor pipelines for data quality/reliability/performance; maintain documentation.
**Reporting & Analytics:** centralized dashboards for ecommerce, customer lifecycle, retail showroom, marketing, product, category; combine behavioral/transactional/marketing/operational data; ecommerce metrics (traffic, product engagement, add-to-cart, checkout behavior, conversion, revenue, AOV, product performance); customer/lifecycle reporting (CRM, email engagement, segmentation, attribution, purchase behavior); identify trends/anomalies/gaps and communicate to stakeholders.
**Ecommerce & Customer Journey Analytics:** analyze customer journey across web/ecommerce/marketing/CRM/retail/post-purchase; identify friction/drop-off (discovery, add-to-cart, checkout, purchase, delivery, retention); CRO opportunities with product teams; support UX/merchandising/personalization; support A/B testing/experimentation (measurement requirements, results, business impact); analyze product/category/customer behavior to lift conversion/AOV/revenue.
**Data Strategy & Business Support:** define KPIs/reporting requirements with teams; consistent business definitions, attribution methodologies, measurement standards; data supporting forecasting, merchandising strategy, inventory optimization, lifecycle marketing, operational planning; support launches; translate business questions into data requirements and recommendations.
