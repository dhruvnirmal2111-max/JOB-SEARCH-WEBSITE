# Sporty Group — Data Analyst

**Company:** Sporty Group — fully remote-first global sports-tech (betting, streaming, fantasy, analytics).
**Location:** Remote-first (core hours 10am-3pm local; flexible outside).

## About the role
Mine petabytes of data for insights that drive core business decisions. Build dashboards used daily by product managers, traders and executives; deliver in-depth reports with freedom on methodology. Analysts also keep the reporting infrastructure stable, ensuring accurate, consistent reporting to stakeholders.

## Our stack
MySQL, Python, Airflow, AWS, Metabase, Redshift, Linux

## What you'll be doing
- Analyse large, complex datasets to find trends, patterns, actionable insights for decisions.
- Design, define and monitor core business KPIs to evaluate product and operational performance.
- Partner with product, marketing and business teams to translate business questions into structured analysis.
- Deliver clear, data-driven insights to stakeholders and shareholders via presentations and reports.
- Develop and maintain dashboards and reporting frameworks that enable self-service analytics.
- Conduct ad hoc analysis for strategic initiatives and emerging needs.
- Ensure data quality and integrity by validating datasets and collaborating with data engineering.

## What you'll bring
- 3+ years in a similar data-driven role.
- Strong experience with KPIs and OKRs.
- Excellent working knowledge of SQL.
- Familiarity with Python and a range of BI/data-visualisation tools.
- Strong knowledge of ETL data pipelines.
- Strong knowledge of A/B testing.
- Familiarity with Airflow.
- Understanding of relational databases and data warehouse principles.
- Strong understanding of sports and gaming beneficial.
- Proactive, self-driven, excellent analytical + communication skills; thrives fast-paced.

## Even better if
- Hands-on machine learning; gaming-industry experience; Python; Airflow + Metabase.
